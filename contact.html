Questions I get asked

What do you actually build?

Web applications end to end: the interface, the API and the database behind it. Most of my work is React or Next.js on the front and Node with PostgreSQL behind it, but I pick what fits the problem rather than what is fashionable.

What's your stack?

TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node, Express, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Prisma, Redis and Tailwind. I'm comfortable reading code I didn't write and working inside an existing codebase.

Are you open to remote work?

Yes. I'm looking for full-time work and can do it remotely or on-site. Tell me your team's working hours and I'll confirm the overlap honestly.

How do you work with a team?

Small pull requests, early review, and questions asked before the work rather than after. I write down what I changed and why, so nobody has to reverse-engineer it later.

What happens when I email you?

I read it the same day and reply within one business day. If I'm not the right person for the role, I'll tell you that instead of leaving you waiting.
