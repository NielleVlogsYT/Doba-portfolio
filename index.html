About me

Most of what I build is the load-bearing, unglamorous kind: a form that holds up when people use it badly, a query that stays fast as the table grows, a deploy that doesn't break on a Sunday.

I started with HTML pages for school projects and stayed for the part where the whole thing has to hold together. I'm looking for a full-time role on a team that ships, where I can own features from the schema up.
